Invite a person to an engine

The invitation carries their permissions. The address is typed once, used to send the email, and never stored: only a hash is kept.

Revoke an invitation

An unused invitation simply stops working. A used one also removes the engine membership and clears the account's generation permission and quota (the account and its episodes stay).
